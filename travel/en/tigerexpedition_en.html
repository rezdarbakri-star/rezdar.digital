Discover Bardiya

Nepal's wildest corner
— and least known

Far to the west, near the border with India, lies a national park most people have never heard of.

  • ~5,574 Wild tigers left in the worldOne of few places where the chance to actually see one is real.
  • -93% Of the tiger's historic range is goneWhat remains are places like Bardiya.
  • 400+ Bird speciesFrom the great hornbill to the dancing florican.
  • On foot Not just from a jeepHere you track tigers on foot with a guide, something very few places in the world allow.
