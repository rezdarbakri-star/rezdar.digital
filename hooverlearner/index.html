📖 Black Rednecks and White Liberals
💰 From The Tax Illusion
In the 1920s, the US cut the top tax rate from 73% to 25%. Revenue from the wealthy…
Revenue nearly DOUBLED. People moved money out of tax shelters.

At 73%, avoidance was rational. At 25%, earning openly beat paying accountants. The number of people reporting high incomes more than tripled. The same pattern repeated under Kennedy and Reagan.

📖 Race and Culture

Same skin color, same country, vastly different outcomes. Culture, selection effects, and specific circumstances explain more than race alone ever could.
